Freelance full stack work on BeeConn, a commercial hive monitoring system.

## What it is

- Beekeepers put scales and a weather station at their hives.
- The web app turns those readings into charts, tables and reports.

## What I built

- Stripe payments, including the package and payment models.
- The data layer, with Entity Framework (code first).
- Access control and user permissions across several parts of the app.
- Charts of the IoT device measurements.

## Built with

C#, .NET, JavaScript, ASP.NET MVC, MySQL.
