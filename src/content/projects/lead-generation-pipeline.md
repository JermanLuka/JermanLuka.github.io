## The problem

- Sales needed qualified leads among UK companies.

## How it works

- A Python pipeline narrows down UK companies step by step.
- It uses public business registers, drive-time maps and job APIs.
- A research skill writes company reports for sales, with links to the
  sources.

## Results

- 185,584 UK companies in.
- 12 qualified leads out.

## Built with

Python, public business registers, drive-time maps, job APIs.
